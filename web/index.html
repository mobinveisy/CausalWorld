01 / RESEARCH QUESTION
“Does accurate future prediction imply that a model has learned a causally editable representation of physics?”

We test a stronger criterion: intervention consistency. The model must change its predicted future for the right physical reason.

02 / METHOD

Observation → latent physics → intervention.

One compact representation is forced to explain multiple contexts and survive counterfactual swaps.

01

Observe

Receive passive trajectories without the hidden mass value as an input.

02

Infer

Encode recurring dynamics into a low-dimensional latent physical state, zphysics.

03

Intervene

Hold the initial scene constant and replace only the inferred physical latent.

04

Measure

Evaluate whether the change in predicted dynamics matches the true counterfactual effect.

CAUSAL EFFECT OBJECTIVE
Leffect = ‖(τ̂cf − τ̂) − (τcf − τ)‖²

Instead of rewarding two independently plausible rollouts, this term directly rewards the change caused by the intervention.
