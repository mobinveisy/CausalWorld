About

What is CausalWorld?

Most models can predict what happens next. CausalWorld asks a deeper question: does accurate prediction mean the model truly understands physics?

Our approach learns a latent physical representation from passive observations and tests it through interventions: keep the initial state fixed, replace only the latent physics, and evaluate whether the predicted motion matches the correct counterfactual world.

Why this project matters

Counterfactual physical reasoning

Not just trajectory prediction — the project evaluates whether changing only latent physics yields the correct changed future.

Public-data driven

Built around Physion++ for hidden physical properties and GAUGE for real-world validation with controlled physical metadata.

Paper-ready reproducibility

Includes baselines, ablations, multi-seed evaluation, Colab notebooks, release docs, and GitHub-ready packaging.
